Discovery Project

For my ECE 1100 Discovery Project I'm building an open-source security camera: a small ESP32-based device that detects when a person is in view, sends me a notification, and saves the footage to an SD card. I'm still building it. The full feature, with photos and results, will be posted here before the Discovery Project Showcase.
