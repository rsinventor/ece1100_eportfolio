Sheet 5 · Career goals

Where I'm headed

Long term, I want to engineer computing systems where hardware and software meet: embedded devices, processors, and the hardware that runs AI.

Almost every project I've built has run into the same wall: the software I wanted didn't fit the hardware I had. BikeWatch only worked because the Kria board had a dedicated deep-learning unit. Designing a processor with Silicon Jackets showed me what happens underneath. I want a career that works across both layers, and this is my plan to get there. I'll revise it each semester.

Roadmap

From here to there

  1. S1
    YEAR 1 · 2026 – 2027 · NOW

    Build the foundation

    • Learn digital design and hardware/software systems in my core classes.
    • Complete a full RTL-to-GDSII flow with Silicon Jackets and learn power electronics with Solar Racing.
    • Start research through the Vertically Integrated Projects (VIP) program. I applied this fall.
    • Land a Summer 2027 internship in software development or embedded systems.
  2. S2
    YEARS 2 – 3 · 2027 – 2029

    Go deeper and specialize

    • Take computer architecture, circuits, and embedded systems. For my threads I plan to pick Computing Hardware & Emerging Architectures for digital design and Circuit Technology for the analog side.
    • Keep doing research through VIP and lead a subteam in Silicon Jackets or Solar Racing.
    • Try out a startup idea through CREATE-X.
    • Complete a co-op or internship on an embedded or silicon engineering team.
  3. S3
    YEAR 4 · 2029 – 2030

    Prove it with a capstone

    • Lead a senior design project that combines custom hardware and software.
    • Choose between a full-time role and Georgia Tech's BS/MS program.
  4. OUT
    AFTER GRADUATION

    Engineer on hardware/software systems

    Within five years of graduating, I want to work as an engineer in chip design, embedded systems, or software at a semiconductor company like NVIDIA or Micron. Within ten, I want to be a technical lead or to have started my own hardware or AI company. Along the way I want to mentor students the way others have mentored me.
